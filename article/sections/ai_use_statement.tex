\section*{Use of AI Tools}
\label{sec:ai-use}

During the preparation of this work, the authors utilized the Claude large language model \cite{anthropic2026claude} as an auxiliary tool for two purposes: (i) assisting in code generation and refactoring during the implementation of the predictive modeling pipeline, and (ii) supporting the drafting and improving the readability of the manuscript. All methodological, architectural, and analytical decisions were made exclusively by the authors. Every piece of code and text generated with the assistance of this tool underwent thorough human review, validation, and necessary modifications prior to integration, and the authors take full responsibility for the technical accuracy, originality, and integrity of the publication.
